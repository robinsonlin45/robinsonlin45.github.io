Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

About the Application

Personal Investment Agent is a private, non-commercial investment research application intended for personal use.

Google Drive Access

With user authorization, the application accesses designated Google Drive files for investment research and saves analysis outputs to authorized folders.

Data Usage and Storage

Data is used for personal investment research and automation. Authorized processing services may include GitHub Actions and Google Drive.

Data Sharing

Investment data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is processed only through services required for the application's operation.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application developer through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
